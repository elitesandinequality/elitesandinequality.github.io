Berlin Network for Elite and Inequality Research

Welcome! We bring together researchers interested in elites and inequality to exchange ideas, discuss ongoing work, and build connections across Berlin’s research community.

Get to know the network ↓

Our core idea

Connecting research on elites and inequality.

We aim to connect researchers across institutions, disciplines, and career stages, bringing different perspectives and methods into conversation. Through talks and informal exchanges, we discuss work in progress, share insights, and develop opportunities for collaboration.

The network meets once per semester.
